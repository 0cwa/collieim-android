Get Collie

Private messages on the Signal network. Stronger protection on your phone.

Collie is a free, open-source Android messenger. Message and call your Signal contacts as you always have, while Collie keeps a tighter hold on what's stored on your device.

Get Collie Meet the community
  • Free and open source
  • Android 8.1 or newer

Talk to anyone on Signal

Message and call your Signal contacts over the same end-to-end encrypted network, with more protection for the messages on your phone.

Built on Signal and Molly

Collie keeps Molly's privacy protections, follows newer Signal releases, and adds features of its own.

Source and security details

Collie's code is public, so people with the expertise to inspect it can see how it works.

  • One APK, with no proprietary libraries, licensed AGPL-3.0.
  • Browse the source code or read the release engineering and signing details.
  • We contribute work back to Molly, for example #852 and #853.

Collie's native libraries have not yet been independently reproduced.

What Collie adds

Locked when you step away

Encrypt your message database with a passphrase you choose. Collie locks itself after a set time, and a lost or stolen phone keeps your conversations encrypted until the passphrase is entered.

  • Passphrase you choose
  • Locks after a set time
  • Stays encrypted if lost or stolen

Less left behind

Sensitive data is cleared from memory, and debug logging can be switched off entirely.

Choose your route

Connect through Tor via Orbot or a SOCKS proxy, set up before you even register.

Notifications without Google

Get message alerts without Google's push service. Collie can keep its own connection open, or use UnifiedPush with a server you trust. Why it matters and why we're working on making it easy.

Close to current Signal

Collie follows newer Signal releases, so recent features and fixes reach you sooner.

Your history on new devices

Link a phone or Android tablet and bring your existing messages along, when your main phone supports it.

Also included: blocking messages and calls from unknown contacts, disappearing call history, and scheduled local backups.

Upcoming features
