Get Collie

Private messages on the Signal network. Stronger protection on your phone.

Collie is an Android app for messaging and calling your Signal contacts.

Get Collie Meet the community
  • Free and open source
  • Android 8.1 or newer

Built on Signal and Molly

Collie keeps Molly’s privacy protections and uses Signal’s end-to-end encrypted network to message your Signal contacts.

Source and security →

What Collie adds

Locked when you step away

Encrypt the messages stored on your phone with a passphrase you choose. Collie locks after a set time, so a lost or stolen phone keeps your conversations encrypted.

Less left behind

Clears sensitive data from memory. Debug logging can be turned off.

Choose your route

Connect through Tor with Orbot or a SOCKS proxy before you register.

Notifications without Google

Get alerts without Google through Collie’s own connection or UnifiedPush. Why it matters and what’s next

Close to current Signal

Get features and fixes from newer Signal releases.

Your history on new devices

Bring your messages to a linked phone or Android tablet, when your main phone supports it.

Also included: Blocking unknown contacts, disappearing call history and scheduled local backups.

Upcoming features

UnifiedPush needs a MollySocket server you run or trust with your account and metadata. We’re simplifying setup and adding support for linked phones.
