<p>Collie is a free and open-source messenger for the Signal network. Talk to your Signal contacts with the same end-to-end encrypted messages and calls, with stronger protection for the data stored on your phone.</p><p>Collie is built on Signal and Molly. It keeps Molly's protections and develops new features independently.</p><p><b>Why Collie over Molly</b></p><ul><li><b>Closer to current Signal:</b> Collie follows newer Signal releases, so you get recent features and fixes sooner.</li><li><b>One fully open-source app:</b> a single APK with no proprietary libraries.</li><li><b>Auditable by design:</b> patch-based releases show exactly what we change, while letting security updates ship fast.</li><li><b>Message history on new devices:</b> linking a device can offer to transfer your existing messages, when your primary device supports it.</li><li><b>Network control from the start:</b> reach proxy settings during registration and directly from Privacy.</li><li><b>Clearer UnifiedPush recovery:</b> a failed registration is shown to you and retried within limits. (UnifiedPush for linked devices will be a future new feature developed by Collie.)</li><li>Installs alongside Signal or Molly, and we contribute work back to Molly.</li></ul><p><b>Protections from Molly</b></p><ul><li>Passphrase encryption for the local message database, with automatic locking</li><li>Clearing sensitive data from memory, and optional debug logging</li><li>Linked devices, including Android tablets</li><li>Blocking messages and calls from unknown contacts</li><li>Disappearing call history and scheduled local backups</li><li>SOCKS proxy and Tor support</li><li>WebSocket and UnifiedPush as alternatives to Google's push service</li></ul><p>If you use UnifiedPush, only use a MollySocket server you trust with your whole account and its metadata. Run your own, and do not use a public one.</p><p>Collie connects to Signal's servers, and Signal's Terms and Privacy Policy apply. Collie is independent of Signal and Molly. Native libraries have not yet been independently reproduced.</p>
