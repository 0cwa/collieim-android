Private messages on the Signal network. Stronger protection on your phone.

Collie is a free, open-source Android messenger. Message and call your Signal contacts as you always have, while Collie keeps a tighter hold on what's stored on your device.

Get Collie View source
  • Free and open source
  • Android 8.1 or newer
  • Installs alongside Signal

Collie talks to the same people over the same end-to-end encrypted Signal network. What changes is how carefully your phone guards everything once it arrives.

Collie is built on Signal and Molly. It keeps Molly's protections, follows newer Signal releases, and develops its own features.

What Collie adds

Locked when you step away

Encrypt your message database with a passphrase you choose. Collie locks itself after a set time, and a lost or stolen phone keeps your conversations encrypted until the passphrase is entered.

Less left behind

Sensitive data is cleared from memory, and debug logging can be switched off entirely.

Choose your route

Connect through Tor via Orbot or a SOCKS proxy, set up before you even register.

Notifications without Google

Use WebSocket or UnifiedPush instead of Google's push service. UnifiedPush needs a MollySocket server you trust, ideally your own. Why it matters

Close to current Signal

Collie follows newer Signal releases, so recent features and fixes reach you sooner.

Your history on new devices

Link a phone or Android tablet and bring your existing messages along, when your main phone supports it.

Also included: blocking messages and calls from unknown contacts, disappearing call history, and scheduled local backups.

Every change is a patch you can read

Each Collie release starts from Signal's source, adds Molly's protections, then applies Collie's own changes as separate patches. Anyone can see exactly what we changed, and security updates can ship quickly.

  • One APK, fully open source, with no proprietary libraries.
  • Licensed AGPL-3.0.
  • We contribute work back to Molly, for example #852 and #853.
  • Release engineering and signing details are public.

Collie's native libraries have not yet been independently reproduced.

Talk to anyone on Signal

Same network

Collie connects to Signal's servers, so your Signal contacts message and call you as usual.

Side by side

Collie has its own package, im.collie.app, so it installs next to Signal or Molly.

One account, two apps

To use one account in both, link Collie as a secondary device. Registering the same number in a second app disconnects the first.

Collie is independent of Signal and Molly. Signal's Terms and Privacy Policy apply when you use its service.
