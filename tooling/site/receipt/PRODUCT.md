# Collie source stories

## Platform
Web, static HTML and CSS. Read mode: help current users understand selected changes and continuing capabilities; prospective users are secondary.

## Product truth
The release feed and app-wide account are distinct scopes. Human-reviewed records and Git-selected frozen evidence supply both. Membership remains unknown. Source identity does not establish behavior, attribution, or shipping approval. Notification delivery and battery effects are unmeasured. Hidden subjects stay hidden.

## Constraints
Extend the established Collie site: Sora, Manrope, Bark/Redwood, Forest, Lichen and Golden. Native disclosures and static links work without JavaScript. No publication, release contract changes, new merge gates or Android behavior changes. Donation is optional and follows the story.

## Source of decisions
The user's audited design brief and implementation handoff supply the audience, goals and boundaries. No additional product interview was conducted: the user explicitly requested implementation using judgment on remaining design defaults. Authority adapters, app-wide baselines and historical story coverage remain undecided.
